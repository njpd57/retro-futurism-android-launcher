# CRT tube effects

Moved from CLAUDE.md. Read before touching `CrtScreen`, `useTubeStore` or `utils/curvature.ts`.

- **The screen is a tube.** `CrtScreen` draws the overlay: scanlines, vignette, flicker, a slow sweep band and optional curvature. All of it is user-toggleable and is turned off or frozen under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Curvature is a black bezel with rounded glass, an inner shadow and a barrel distortion: an SVG `feDisplacementMap` filter on `.tube` whose map is drawn on a canvas (`utils/curvature.ts`, pure and tested). Settings `curvatureBend` / `curvatureCorners` / `curvatureShade` (0..100) tune it. The filter moves what is painted, not hit-testing, so `CrtScreen` redirects `click` (and the select sound) to the element under the finger with `correctPoint` + `elementFromPoint`; long press, window drags and canvas games are not corrected, which is why the bend is capped (`MAX_BEND_K`). Keep the bend small and test touches near the edges on the device. `useTubeStore` drives three more tube effects drawn in `CrtScreen`: a degauss pulse (`degauss()`, a second SVG filter `#crt-degauss` chained after the bend while it plays; fired from settings and by every phosphor change), the switch off / on (`settings.tubePower`): the picture folds *before* an app is launched (`collapseThen`, called by `useLaunchStore.launch`, which therefore delays `requestLaunchApp` by ~0.4 s) and flashes back on at `afterResume`; `beforePause` is too late to be seen, and `visibilitychange` or a 2.5 s timer bring the picture back so the screen can never stay dark; the degauss is the `> desmagnetizar` command and also runs by itself every 20-60 minutes of visible screen (`settings.autoDegauss`, `utils/degauss.ts`) and an optional phosphor persistence (`settings.phosphorBurn`, `utils/burnIn.ts`, a ghost of at most 3 % on the frame lines only, never over text).
