# Game engines (AISLAMIENTO, MARINES, wire)

Moved from CLAUDE.md. The games are finished: read only if the user asks for a change to one. Plans: `AISLAMIENTO.md`, `AISLAMIENTO_LIBRE.md`, `MARINES.md`.

- `src/games/`: pure, tested logic of the minigames (`tracker.ts` and `reroute.ts` with injected RNG, `nostromo.ts` with `reduce(state, input)`).
- **AISLAMIENTO** (full-screen survival game; all phases done: a five-deck campaign and an endless mode, see the next paragraph; the plan, state and how to verify are in `docs/AISLAMIENTO.md`): pure logic in `src/games/isolation/` (`grid.ts` maps drawn as text, BFS, sound through doors, line of sight; `alien.ts` the creature's senses and modes; `game.ts` `reduce(state, input)` / `step(state, dt, rng)`; `view.ts` the first-person wireframe as flat shapes, built on the shared `src/games/wire/` engine (below); `crafting.ts` the bag, recipes and limits, `decks.ts`, `story.ts`), UI in `src/components/isolation/` (`IsolationGame` the screen and loop, `IsolationView` the canvas, `IsolationControls` an analog joystick (`stickAxes` of MARINES) plus the tap keys, which act on `touchstart`; a drag on the view turns, with `sensitivity` in `widget.isolation.prefs`, `IsolationBag` the [OBJETOS] panel, which doesn't stop the clock), opened with `> aislamiento` through `useIsolationStore` as one more overlay in `App.vue`. The terminal save and the game suspended on leaving are `widget.isolation.save` / `widget.isolation.suspend` (`save.ts` snapshots only what changes, is versioned, and validates on read: an old version reads as no save). Maps carry loot (`s` scrap, `w` wires, `c` chems, `f` flamethrower) and decks may declare `dark` rectangles: unlit cells show only one step away (`view.ts` skips them, except terminals, the exit and power cells, which glow) and the creature's sight drops to `ALIEN.darkSight` while the player is unlit; a flare lights the cells within 4 steps. The keys are DOM buttons on purpose: `CrtScreen` corrects `click` for the curvature but not touches on a canvas. **Movement is free, not by cells:** the player, the creature and Ash have continuous `x, y` (plus `yaw` / `heading`) in a world whose maps are scaled 2x2 by `scale.ts` (`scaleMap`, applied by `deckFor` and the generator); distances are in cells, speeds in cells/s, and `hints.ts` / `plan.ts` divide by `SCALE`. Collision is `wire/physics.ts` (shared with MARINES), sight and hearing are `senses.ts`, saves are `v: 4` (older ones read as no save). The details are in `docs/AISLAMIENTO_LIBRE.md`.
- **AISLAMIENTO campaign and endless mode** (phases 4-5): `decks.ts` holds the five decks of `CAMPAIGN` (C tutorial, B CONDUCTOS dark with the flamethrower, LAB ASH, MAINT fallos, HANGAR with the countdown and Jones) as text maps plus a `DeckDef` (`terminals`: what each `T` does besides saving, `blackout`, `countdownMs`), and `deckFor(id)` also resolves generated ids `inf:<seed>:<level>` from `generate.ts` (maze with loops and rooms, always solvable, hazards as the level grows; a save keeps only the id). Map letters added to `grid.ts`: `H` Ash, `X` a door without power, `~` a steam leak, `j` Jones. `android.ts` is Ash (sees along a line, slower than the player, goes through nothing shut, ignores noise and fire, invisible to the tracker; a terminal with `ashOff` switches him off); `game.ts` adds the steam cycle (`steaming`), the `blackout` sync, locked doors opened by a terminal with `opens`, the self-destruct `countdownMs`, Jones (`jones`, meows), the crew logs read (`logs`), the plan sent by any terminal (`mapped`) and `death` (`alien` | `ash` | `blast`); `terminalTask(s)` says what [USAR] will do at a terminal. `difficulty.ts` (FÁCIL / NORMAL / DIFÍCIL: creature pace and sight, countdown, a decoy and a flare as a gift per deck). `campaign.ts` (`startCampaign`, `startInfinite`, `advance` carries the bag, Jones, logs and counters to the next deck, `deckScore`), `progress.ts` (the archive across runs, 10 achievements, `record(progress, event)` pure), `logs.ts` (12 original crew logs and the endings), `plan.ts` (the deck as text for the map), `hints.ts` (`hintFor`, `describeSave` for `/pista` and `/partida`), `story.ts` (MOTHER's lines). Saves are `v: 3`; the older ones read as no save. UI: `IsolationGame` runs the screens (title, difficulty, briefing, playing, paused, report, ending), `IsolationMap` (the plan), `IsolationArchive` (logs, achievements, stats), `IsolationWidget` (panel). The endless mode opens when the campaign has been finished (`infiniteUnlocked`). `/jugar`, `/partida`, `/pista` are MOTHER slash commands that run on the phone.
- **`src/games/wire/`** (shared first-person wireframe engine, pure, tested; used by AISLAMIENTO and MARINES): `camera.ts` (`createCamera` projection and clipping, `visibleCells`, the `Shape` type, `depthAlpha`), `level.ts` (`buildLevel`: floor, lamps, walls, doors and signs far to near, with per-cell hooks `light` / `ground` / `wall` / `stand` for what is particular to each game), `occlusion.ts` (`castRay` DDA, a per-column `depthBuffer` and `openSpans`: sprites at continuous positions are drawn clipped to where no nearer wall hides them), `creature.ts` (the silhouette). The canvas side is `src/components/wire/drawShapes.ts` (filled faces, double-stroke glow, tilted text, `clip` spans; line widths per tag in `STYLE`). Refactors of `buildView` are checked against a fingerprint of its output over many poses of CUBIERTA C, which must stay identical.
- **MARINES** (full-screen action game in the style of *Aliens: Colonial Marines*; phases 0-5 done (squad of two marines, grenades, shotgun / smartgun / flamethrower, runners, facehuggers, spitters, acid, sentry turrets and the door welder in `defenses.ts`, four arenas, per-arena records in `report.ts`, a suspended game in `save.ts`, a four-mission campaign with difficulty in `campaign.ts` (the last one fights a queen in the NIDO arena, whose caverns are dark: only the shoulder lamp, `view.LAMP`, shows things there), 14 achievements in `achievements.ts`, cumulative stats in `report.ts`, and a `MarinesWidget` for the dashboard), the plan, state and how to verify are in `docs/MARINES.md`): pure logic in `src/games/marines/` (`map.ts` arena drawn as text with spawn grilles `V`, ammo `a` and medkits `+`; `physics.ts` circle movement that slides along walls; `weapons.ts` aim assist, spread by heat and `resolveShot`; `enemies.ts` the distance field creatures flow down, separation and wound-up strikes; `waves.ts`; `game.ts` `step(state, dt, controls, rng)` / `act(state, action)` working on a copy, in slices of at most 50 ms; `radio.ts` MANDO's lines; `view.ts` creature sprites clipped by the depth buffer, tracers, sparks), UI in `src/components/marines/` (`MarinesGame` screens, loop and keyboard, `MarinesView` the canvas with the rifle, its inverse-video round counter, the crosshair and the motion tracker, `MarinesControls` the joystick, FUEGO and RECARGA), opened with `> marines` through `useMarinesStore` (`open`, `widget.marines.records`, `widget.marines.prefs`: drag sensitivity and 30/60 fps). Movement is free: an analog **joystick** moves (`stick.ts`, pure and tested: the thumb may land anywhere on the zone and what counts is how far it is dragged, with a dead zone and a circular clamp, so a light push walks slowly) and **a drag on the view turns the player**; each follows its own `Touch.identifier` and uses how far it moved, so the curvature of the CRT doesn't matter, and FUEGO is a hold key that uses `touchstart` with `preventDefault`, so two thumbs and a third finger work at once. WASD and the arrow keys still work on a desktop. The weapon cooldown carries over between slices so the cadence doesn't depend on the frame rate. Only the game's own touches are handled by it: panels cover the whole screen, not just the view.
